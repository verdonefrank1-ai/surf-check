Where the numbers come from. Waves, swell and water temperature: Open-Meteo Marine (model data, updated hourly). Wind and air: Open-Meteo weather models. Tides: NOAA tide predictions for the nearest ocean inlet station. "Now" is the model's estimate for the current hour, not a buoy reading.

Real buoy readings: NDBC 44025 (south of Fire Island) · NDBC 44065 (NY Harbor entrance) · NDBC 44017 (Montauk) · NWS surf zone forecast

How the rating works. Swell height and period are turned into an estimated wave face height for each break, adjusted for which way the beach faces. That is scored against the size you picked, then marked down for onshore or cross-shore wind and nudged for tide. Crowd levels are typical patterns, not live counts. Sandbars move, so treat this as a shortlist and check a cam or the beach before you commit.
