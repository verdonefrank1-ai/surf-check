Ticker Lab is an educational tool, not financial advice. Signals are simple rules of thumb applied to public data; they are often wrong. Option prices here are Black–Scholes estimates, not quotes. Quotes via Finnhub, daily history via Twelve Data. Data can be delayed or wrong; check your broker before trading.

Settings & data

Both keys are free. They are stored only in this browser (localStorage) and sent only to the provider they belong to. Without a Finnhub key the app runs on simulated demo data.

Free-tier limits: Finnhub 60 calls/min, Twelve Data 8 calls/min & 800/day. Price history is cached for 6 hours so the first load of a large watchlist is the slowest.
